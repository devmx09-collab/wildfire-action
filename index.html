Wildfire Action

A layered way to help towns resist wildfire: a non-combustible perimeter, a managed buffer, and ember-resistant buildings.

A fully hardened town against wildfire.

1. Non-combustible perimeter

Use gravel, stone, compacted mineral soil, or a service road to slow fire spread.

2. Managed buffer zone

Reduce fuel with spaced trees, trimmed grass, and no brush piles or dry debris.

3. Hardened buildings

Use ember-resistant vents, clean gutters, sealed openings, and fire-resistant materials.

Why it matters

Wildfires often destroy towns through embers and rapid fire spread. Layered protection can reduce ignition risk and improve emergency access.
